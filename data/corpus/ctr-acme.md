# Master Transport Agreement - Acme Housewares

## Parties and term

This agreement is between Meridian Freight and Acme Housewares. The initial term is 12 months from the effective date and renews annually unless either party gives 90 days' written notice.

## Service levels

Meridian commits to an on-time delivery rate of 94 percent measured monthly. Service credits of 2 percent of monthly charges apply for each full percentage point below target, up to 10 percent.

## Commercial terms

Invoices are payable net 30 days. Acme Housewares receives a volume rebate of 1.0 percent of annual freight spend above the agreed threshold. The approved credit limit is 100,000 USD.

## Liability

Meridian's aggregate liability under this agreement is capped at 1,000,000 USD per contract year, excluding cargo liability governed by the damaged freight claims procedure.
