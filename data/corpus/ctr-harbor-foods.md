# Master Transport Agreement - Harbor Foods Co-op

## Parties and term

This agreement is between Meridian Freight and Harbor Foods Co-op. The initial term is 24 months from the effective date and renews annually unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.

## Service levels

Meridian commits to an on-time delivery rate of 95 percent measured monthly. Service credits of 2 percent of monthly charges apply for each full percentage point below target, up to 10 percent.

## Commercial terms

Invoices are payable net 60 days. Harbor Foods Co-op receives a volume rebate of 1.5 percent of annual freight spend above the agreed threshold. The approved credit limit is 400,000 USD.

## Liability

Meridian's aggregate liability under this agreement is capped at 250,000 USD per contract year, excluding cargo liability governed by the damaged freight claims procedure.
